A good machine concept does not start with CAD.

It starts with the process.

What needs to happen?

What can remain manual?

What really needs automation?

Which option is cheaper in the short term?

Which one makes more sense over the full life of the process?

These questions often matter more than the first geometry.

Sometimes the best solution is a complete machine.

Sometimes it is a fixture, a sensor, a mechanical stop, or simply a better sequence.

More automation is not automatically a better concept.

CAD, simulation, and AI can all help.

They are especially useful for exploring alternatives, challenging assumptions, and finding questions we may not think of immediately.

But the first step is still understanding the process.

The machine should follow the process, not the other way around.
