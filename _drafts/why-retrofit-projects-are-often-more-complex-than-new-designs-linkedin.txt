Retrofit projects often look simpler than new machine designs.

After all, part of the machine already exists.

But that is exactly what makes them difficult.

You inherit existing geometry, interfaces, components, operator habits and maintenance constraints.

Sometimes you also inherit incomplete or outdated documentation.

A new machine can often be designed around the process.

A retrofit has to work around both the process and the machine that is already there.

The technical solution itself may be simple.

The difficult part is making it fit reliably into an existing system.
