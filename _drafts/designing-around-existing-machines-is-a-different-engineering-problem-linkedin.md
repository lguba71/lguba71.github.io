Designing around an existing machine is not the same as designing a new one.

With a new machine, mounting points, space and access can often be designed around the solution.

With an existing machine, most of those decisions have already been made.

The machine itself becomes part of the design specification.

A component may fit in CAD but still be difficult to install.

A fastener may be visible but impossible to reach with a real tool.

A sensor may fit but have no practical cable route.

A new mechanism may work perfectly but block maintenance access.

Retrofit design is not only about making something fit geometrically.

It has to fit into the complete machine, its operation, maintenance and existing constraints.
