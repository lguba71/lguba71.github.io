A CAD model can look correct and still be difficult to manufacture or assemble.

One common reason is simple:

the tool cannot reach the feature properly.

A screw may be correctly selected, but placed where the assembly tool cannot be used comfortably.

A pocket may look clean in the model, but require an unstable machining setup.

A feature may be dimensionally correct, but difficult to inspect.

Tool access is not only a machining issue.

It also affects assembly, inspection, adjustment, cleaning and future service.

Good mechanical design is not only about placing features correctly.

It is also about making them reachable.
